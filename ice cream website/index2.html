Our Story

Born from a love for Indian flavours and frozen dreams.

RAS started with a simple idea — take the flavours we grew up loving and turn them into something cold, creamy and completely irresistible.

From kesar and pista to mango, chai and gulab jamun, every scoop is inspired by India's rich food culture and made fresh in small batches.
